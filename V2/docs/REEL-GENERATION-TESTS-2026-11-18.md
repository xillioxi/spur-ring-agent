## Prompts

### 1. Product reveal

A luxury product reel in one macro studio take. Open tight on a bead of water on the brushed crown of a sapphire-blue wristwatch. Pull focus from the bead to the dial while the camera slides back and the turntable rotates slightly. End on the same whole watch, sharp on black slate, with a slender moving highlight. Photographic, restrained, no titles.

### 2. Travel match cut

A Hong Kong travel reel in exactly two shots. Start on a rainy neon-lit Kowloon pavement at night, with a round red umbrella seen directly from above. Push in until the red circle fills the image. Hard match cut to a round red sun in the same position, then pull back to reveal Victoria Harbour and the Star Ferry at dawn. Real travel cinematography, no text.

### 3. Coffee ASMR

A photoreal cafe ASMR reel. Locked close side view of a clear glass filled with ice and white milk. A metal pitcher pours one thin dark espresso stream; brown tendrils bloom down between distinct ice cubes, then the stream stops. Hear the pour and a single ice clink in sync, no music, no dialogue. Hold on the marbling.

### 4. UGC demo

A casual front-camera UGC reel in a sunlit kitchen. One adult woman looks into the lens and says exactly “Watch it snap shut.” She holds an open yellow lunchbox at chest level, then presses its blue lid closed with one hand until the latch clicks. Hold on the shut box and her smile. Natural phone footage, audible line, consistent hands and box, no subtitles.

### 5. Sports tracking

A single side-tracking slow-motion skate reel at an empty concrete skatepark. Full body and board in frame. One skater rolls left to right toward a low red curb, pops one ollie cleanly over it, lands with both feet on the same deck on the far side, and continues right. Camera pans smoothly; no cuts or captions.

### 6. Stop-motion loop

Locked overhead clay stop-motion on cream paper. Start with a single red clay ball in the middle. In chunky handmade increments it sprouts five red petals to become a flower with a red center, briefly holds, then the petals retract and it returns to the identical ball in the identical position for a looping reel. Fingerprints visible. No hands, no camera motion, no writing.

### 7. Typography card

A minimalist motion-design event reel. Cobalt blue background; two large flat white lines of bold type slide into alignment and hold perfectly still and legible for the final two seconds. Exact first line: NIGHT MARKET. Exact second line: FRIDAY 8 PM. One small yellow circle rolls underneath. Straight-on, no other words, no additional graphics.

### 8. Science explainer

An educational reel, hand-painted scientific cutaway, fixed side view. One bean seed lies just beneath a brown soil line. Show it crack, then a single white root grows DOWN into soil. Only after that a green shoot curls UP and breaks above the soil line; two small green leaves unfold in the air. Keep the seed anchored and the root visible. No labels or text.

### 9. Surreal POV transition

A cinematic photoreal first-person surreal reel. Camera flies inches above a giant black-and-white chessboard between tall chess pieces. One square hinges open and the camera dives THROUGH the square, visibly passes its wooden underside, falls toward a mossy forest floor below and comes to rest looking along the ground. Continuous forward movement with no cut or dissolve.

### 10. Fashion transformation

A fashion transition reel, fixed full-body portrait framing against a plain mint studio wall. One adult dancer wearing a white T-shirt, blue jeans and white shoes turns clockwise. At the exact moment their back faces camera, cut on motion to a vivid orange tailored suit. Finish the same turn facing the lens. Same face, height, short dark hair, shoes, floor marks and lighting throughout. No titles.
